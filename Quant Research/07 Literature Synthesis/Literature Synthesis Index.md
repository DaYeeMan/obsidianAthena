# Literature Synthesis Index

This folder captures cross-paper connections and framework candidates that are broader than any single paper or strategy note.

## Core Views

- [[Framework Candidate Registry]] — reusable research frameworks created from multiple sources.
- [[Open Research Questions]] — unresolved questions that should guide future source collection and backtests.
- [[Concept Bridge Maps/Concept Bridge Map Index|Concept Bridge Maps]] — mechanism-level maps across asset classes, methods, and outside domains.

## Purpose

Daily collection is for triage. Weekly synthesis is for connection-making.

A good synthesis item should:

1. connect at least two notes, papers, or methods;
2. propose a falsifiable framework or research question;
3. specify what data/backtest evidence would confirm or reject it;
4. identify practical implementation limits, costs, and failure modes;
5. preserve useful concepts from other domains only when they can improve quant research design.
