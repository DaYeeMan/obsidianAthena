# Concept Bridge Map Index

Use concept bridge maps to connect mechanisms across papers, asset classes, and outside domains.

## Candidate Bridge Types

- **Method transfer**: e.g., epidemiological regime/change-point tools applied to market contagion or volatility states.
- **Validation transfer**: e.g., causal-inference or decision-theory evaluation applied to strategy backtests.
- **Mechanism analogy**: e.g., ecology/network resilience applied to crypto dependency networks.
- **Risk-model bridge**: e.g., heavy-tail simulation + portfolio decision regret + cost/capacity models.
