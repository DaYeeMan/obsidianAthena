# Framework Candidate Registry

This registry tracks multi-paper / multi-domain frameworks that may become reusable research programs, validation standards, or strategy families.

## Recent Framework Updates

- **2026-07-27:** [[Susceptible Reservoir Architectures for Regime-Conditional Volatility Forecasting]] strengthens the Distributional-forecast-first ML and Regime-conditional distributional strategy evaluation frameworks by adding an anchored-residual rule: nonlinear volatility models should start from AR/HAR/GARCH/EWMA anchors, add bounded residual corrections only when per-asset QLIKE improves, and then prove downstream net utility after turnover/costs.
- **2026-07-27:** [[Crypto Bubble Diagnostics - LPPL and PSY Tests for Bitcoin and Ether]] adds an outdated-watch crypto crash-risk label candidate: LPPL/PSY bubble diagnostics may be useful as historical stress labels, but modern crypto strategy use must beat simple realized-volatility, drawdown, trend, and funding filters across post-2018 market structure.
- **2026-07-26:** [[2026-07-26 Weekly Quant Synthesis and Strategy Decay Review]] strengthens the Validation-budget and value-of-information audit triage framework with two adjacent-domain imports: finite-sample excluded-pool coverage audits (arXiv:2607.21480v1) and pre-treatment path reference inference for DID/event studies (arXiv:2607.21312v1). Use these to audit rejected candidates, filtered option quotes, discarded source leads, and event-study pre-periods before claiming a research screen is clean.
- **2026-07-26:** [[2026-07-26 Weekly Quant Synthesis and Strategy Decay Review]] reaffirms the standard cost/regime/liquidity/decision audit block as the top coding-support artifact and expands the Microstructure-conditioned decay and liquidity-state validation framework toward mechanism-specific execution states: ADL/liquidation stress, anchor failure, AMM/settlement loss, stale block state, gas, failed transactions, MEV/sandwich exposure, and route-support shortfall.
- **2026-07-24:** [[DEX Routing Sub-Optimality - Information-Timely Gas-Aware AMM Execution]] strengthens the Microstructure-conditioned decay and liquidity-state validation framework by adding information-timely AMM route-quality diagnostics: support-constrained/full-venue/gas-aware shortfall, stale-state delay, sandwich/MEV flags, gas, failed transactions, and trade-size buckets. Use as an execution-cost audit for DEX/DeFi backtests, not standalone alpha.
- **2026-07-24:** [[Pathwise Roughness of Bitcoin Realized Volatility]] strengthens the Regime-conditional distributional strategy evaluation and Distributional-forecast-first ML strategy evaluation frameworks as a measurement-sensitive BTC volatility-state covariate. Require incremental downstream utility versus EWMA/HAR/GARCH, realized-volatility, funding, drawdown, and venue-stress baselines before any risk-throttle use.

## Status Legend

- **Active**: worth developing or validating.
- **Watch**: promising but needs more evidence or clearer implementation path.
- **Backtest-ready**: framework has a minimum viable test design.
- **Rejected**: connection was weak, non-falsifiable, or not useful after review.
- **Foundational**: keep as a reusable research lens, not necessarily a strategy.

## Maintenance Rules

- Add only frameworks that connect multiple sources or transfer a mechanism across domains.
- Do not add vague themes like "use ML for crypto" unless they become falsifiable.
- Prefer frameworks that improve strategy design, model validation, cost modeling, regime handling, or portfolio construction.
- Record why a connection may fail; novelty without falsifiability is not useful.
