# Framework Candidate Registry

This registry tracks multi-paper / multi-domain frameworks that may become reusable research programs, validation standards, or strategy families.

## Recent Framework Updates

- **2026-08-11:** [[Robustness or Crowding - Experimental Design for Trading Strategy Capacity]] adds a capacity/crowding identification layer to cost-aware decision-process diagnostics and microstructure-conditioned decay validation: backtests should not infer capacity from full-period Sharpe or same-date implementation comparisons alone; they need deployment/participation variation, liquidity/depth/open-interest/borrow/funding crowding proxies, delayed-impact attribution, and baseline comparisons versus spread, volatility, and turnover.
- **2026-08-11:** [[Cross-Venue Agreement Is Not Price Discovery - Equity-Perpetual Oracle Disclosure]] strengthens the prediction-market/event-linked derivative design-risk framework: cross-venue agreement, lead-lag, or information-share estimates in closed-window equity-perps and oracle-linked venues are not price-discovery evidence unless oracle disclosure or cash-reopen validation breaks the self/peer-reference equivalence class.
- **2026-08-11:** [[Marginally Useful - Conformal Prediction Information Gap]] reinforces the distributional-forecast-first ML framework by separating conformal marginal coverage from forecast quality: conformal sizing, Kelly, or kill-switch modules should report residual-feature dependence, interval sharpness, state-conditional coverage, exchangeability/dependence checks, and action attribution before promotion.
- **2026-08-11:** [[Bias-Robust Causal Inference for Panel Data]] adds a counterfactual-error layer to event-study validation: PEAD/accounting, AMM protocol-fee, commodity-shock, crypto venue, and prediction-market studies should report placebo residuals, factor-rank/model sensitivity, counterfactual-error-aware intervals, and post-cost PnL before treating effects as tradable.

- **2026-08-10:** [[Beyond Co-Movement - Factor-Graph Portfolio Diversification]] and [[Certified High-Dimensional Wasserstein Robust Portfolio Optimization]] extend the decision-aware covariance/risk-model benchmark suite: exposure-local graph estimators and certified Wasserstein-DRO should be tested only as governed allocation modules versus equal weight, inverse-vol, risk parity, Ledoit-Wolf/nonlinear shrinkage, factor/sector covariance, EWMA, fixed-radius DRO, and heavy-tail robust baselines after turnover, concentration, and cost stress.
- **2026-08-10:** [[Treasury Yield TVTP Regime Models - Robust Regime Characterization]] strengthens the macro/rates regime-validation branch: filtered Markov-switching Treasury states may improve validation buckets and action attribution, but complex transition-probability models should not be promoted as short-horizon alpha when simpler VIX/yield-curve/realized-volatility/drawdown filters explain the same decisions.

- **2026-08-09:** [[Risk in a Data-Rich Model]] adds a macro-financial tail-state validation layer to strategy risk throttles: short-vol, allocation, anomaly, and crypto risk-control backtests should bucket losses, recovery, costs, and missed-rebound action attribution by lagged financial-conditions/inflation/credit/liquidity states and require incremental value versus VIX, realized-volatility, drawdown, and credit-spread filters before using macro states for sizing.

## Status Legend

- **Active**: worth developing or validating.
- **Watch**: promising but needs more evidence or clearer implementation path.
- **Backtest-ready**: framework has a minimum viable test design.
- **Rejected**: connection was weak, non-falsifiable, or not useful after review.
- **Foundational**: keep as a reusable research lens, not necessarily a strategy.
