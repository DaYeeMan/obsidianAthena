# Datacenter Power, Cooling, and Construction

## Role in chain
Converts chip/server availability into usable AI compute capacity. Can become the binding bottleneck through grid interconnection, transformers, switchgear, cooling, land, water, permitting, and skilled construction labor.

## Key entities
Vertiv, Eaton, Schneider Electric, ABB, Siemens, MOD, PWR, Prysmian/PRY.MI, Atkore/ATKR until acquisition close, BW where generation-equipment scope is confirmed, IREN, GLXY, APLD, CLSK, MARA, HUT, CORZ, BTDR/Bitdeer Tydal, AEP/AEP Texas where interconnection exposure is material, NAVER/GAK Sejong, SK Telecom, ACS/Turner, BlackRock/GIP/Brookfield-linked platforms where material, Base Electron/Babcock & Wilcox where APLD power-generation details develop, datacenter REITs/developers, utilities, grid operators.

## Signals to monitor
- Utility interconnection queues and power purchase agreements.
- Large datacenter campus permits, delays, and cancellations.
- Transformer/switchgear lead times.
- Liquid-cooling adoption and thermal-density constraints.
- Nuclear/gas/renewable power procurement linked to AI datacenters.
