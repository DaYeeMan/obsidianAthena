# Datacenter Power, Cooling, and Construction

## Role in chain
Converts chip/server availability into usable AI compute capacity. Can become the binding bottleneck through grid interconnection, transformers, switchgear, cooling, land, water, permitting, and skilled construction labor.

## Key entities
Vertiv, Eaton, Schneider Electric, ABB, Siemens, MOD, PWR, IREN, GLXY, APLD, CLSK, MARA, HUT, AEP/AEP Texas where interconnection exposure is material, NAVER/GAK Sejong, SK Telecom, ACS/Turner, BlackRock/GIP/Brookfield-linked platforms where material, datacenter REITs/developers, utilities, grid operators.

## Signals to monitor
- Utility interconnection queues and power purchase agreements.
- Large datacenter campus permits, delays, and cancellations.
- Transformer/switchgear lead times.
- Liquid-cooling adoption and thermal-density constraints.
- Nuclear/gas/renewable power procurement linked to AI datacenters.

## Current assessment

Large AI datacenter commitments are now appearing as primary-sourced hyperscaler capex/lease disclosures, long-duration MW leases, AI cloud services contracts, delivery milestones, power-rich site-control transactions, project-finance pricing, sovereign AI factory programs, and infrastructure-platform launches with named construction capacity. NVIDIA's Korea releases add planned 200 MW and up-to-2 GW sovereign AI factory signals, while Alphabet's $80.6B H1 2026 capex and $85.2B of non-commenced data-center lease payments sit alongside the TeraWulf/Anthropic 401 MW commitment, Galaxy/CoreWeave 133 MW delivered critical IT load and Helios Phase II $3.507B / 9.875% note pricing, Applied Digital Delta Forge 1 / Polaris Forge 3 / Delta Forge 2 hyperscaler leases totaling 810 MW of critical IT load across three historical events, CleanSpark 175 MW Sandersville lease, Hut 8 352 MW Beacon Point Phase 2 lease / 1,000 MW campus commercialization, IREN $2.8B AI cloud contracts tied to 480 MW 2026 delivery and 1.2 GW 2027 target, Applied Digital 75 MW Polaris Forge 1 operational delivery, MARA/HIF Matagorda 2 GW powered-site pipeline, and Coravel/ACS/GIP 140 MW DFW hyperscaler agreement. Together these support treating power/site readiness, financing cost, and electrical/cooling/construction capacity as binding deployment layers for frontier AI compute. Execution risk remains high for future phases until LOIs become binding, financing closes, equipment, interconnection, tenant leases/contracts, and commissioning milestones are visible; nonbinding/LOI and secondary-source platform claims should be weighted below SEC-filed lease, contract, hyperscaler capex/lease, closed financing, or delivered-load evidence.
