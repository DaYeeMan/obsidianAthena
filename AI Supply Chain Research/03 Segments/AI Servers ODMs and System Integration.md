# AI Servers, ODMs, and System Integration

## Role in chain
Integrates accelerators, CPUs, memory, networking, power delivery, cooling, racks, firmware, and service/support into deployable AI systems.

## Key entities
SMCI, DELL, HPE, Lenovo, Quanta, Wistron, Wiwynn, Inventec, Foxconn/FII.

## Signals to monitor
- AI server backlog, margin, customer concentration, and inventory commentary.
- Liquid-cooled rack shipments.
- ODM revenue mix shifts and hyperscaler order cadence.
- Shortages in power supplies, substrates, networking, or thermal components.
