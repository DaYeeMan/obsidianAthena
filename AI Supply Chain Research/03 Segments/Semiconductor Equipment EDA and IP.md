# Semiconductor Equipment, EDA, and IP

## Role in chain
Enables leading-edge manufacturing, packaging capacity, inspection/test, and design productivity. Order patterns can lead capacity additions.

## Key entities
ASML, AMAT, LRCX, TEL, KLAC, ASMI, BESI, Teradyne, Advantest, Synopsys, Cadence, Siemens EDA, ARM.

## Signals to monitor
- Foundry/memory capex revisions.
- Advanced packaging equipment orders.
- Export-control changes affecting China equipment demand.
- EDA/IP demand tied to custom AI silicon.
