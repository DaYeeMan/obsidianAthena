# HBM and Memory

## Role in chain
HBM bandwidth and capacity are critical inputs for AI accelerators. DRAM/NAND cycles also affect server BOM costs and memory-vendor capex.

## Key entities
SK Hynix, Samsung Electronics, Micron, equipment/material suppliers linked to HBM and advanced packaging.

## Signals to monitor
- HBM qualification status with accelerator vendors.
- HBM3E/HBM4 capacity, yields, pricing, and capex.
- TSV/packaging constraints and wafer allocation.
- Memory mix shift away from commodity DRAM/NAND.

## Current assessment

Primary evidence now supports treating HBM as an active supplier-fundamental bottleneck, not only a platform roadmap issue. SK Hynix disclosed Q2 2026 record memory economics, HBM4 mass shipments beginning in Q2, HBM4E sample completion in H1, long-term agreements with around 10 key customers, and demand exceeding supply capabilities. Samsung disclosed record Memory revenue/profit, scaled-up HBM4 sales, first HBM4E samples to major customers, and continuing supply constraints despite production increases. Micron remains a production-stage HBM4 supplier for NVIDIA Vera Rubin, while SK Hynix is also a named long-term NVIDIA next-generation AI memory/HBM partner for planned Vera Rubin-scale AI factories. Actual vendor share, allocation, pricing, yields, and margin durability remain the key unknowns.
