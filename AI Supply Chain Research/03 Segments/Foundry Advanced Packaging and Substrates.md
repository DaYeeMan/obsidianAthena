# Foundry, Advanced Packaging, and Substrates

## Role in chain
Leading-edge wafers and advanced packaging convert chip designs into shippable accelerators. ABF substrates, interposers, and packaging equipment can bottleneck shipments.

## Key entities
TSMC, Samsung Foundry, Intel Foundry, ASE, Amkor, Ibiden, Unimicron, AT&S, Shinko, Ajinomoto-related substrate ecosystem.

## Signals to monitor
- CoWoS/SoIC/FoWLP capacity expansion and utilization.
- Leading-edge wafer capacity and allocation.
- ABF substrate lead times/pricing.
- Packaging equipment orders and bottleneck relief.
