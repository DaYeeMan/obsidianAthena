# Networking, Optics, and Interconnect

## Role in chain
Large AI clusters require high-bandwidth low-latency fabrics; inference scaling can intensify East-West and North-South traffic needs.

## Key entities
NVDA, AVGO, MRVL, ANET, CSCO, COHR, LITE, Fabrinet, 3M/MMM where physical optical-connectivity adoption becomes material, and optical module makers where investable/trackable.

## Signals to monitor
- Ethernet vs InfiniBand adoption.
- Switch ASIC cycles and AI back-end networking wins.
- 800G/1.6T optics demand, DSPs, lasers, packaging, thermal limits.
- Copper vs optical interconnect transitions.
- Physical optical-connector deployment speed, contamination tolerance, cleaning/inspection burden, and standardization signals such as EBO MSA adoption.

## Current assessment

Current evidence supports monitoring Ethernet/optics and physical optical connectivity as potential AI cluster value-shift layers. Meta and Microsoft's primary capex/lease/commitment filings confirm large network-infrastructure and server/network purchase demand at the hyperscaler level, Arista's 1.6T portfolio is product-cycle evidence, and the 3M/Microsoft EBO announcement is an early named-hyperscaler deployment signal. Evidence is still not quantified enough to prove supplier-specific backlog, optical-port counts, or a broad networking bottleneck.
