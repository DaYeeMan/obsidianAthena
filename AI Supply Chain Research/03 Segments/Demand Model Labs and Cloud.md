## Current assessment

Frontier model-lab, GPU-cloud, hyperscaler, and sovereign/Gulf AI demand is converting into policy-backed procurement pathways, long-duration datacenter leasing commitments, delivered capacity, and competition for power-rich site pipelines. The TeraWulf/Anthropic lease, Galaxy/CoreWeave Helios delivery, Applied Digital/unnamed hyperscaler Delta Forge 2 lease, CleanSpark/confidential global technology tenant Sandersville lease, Applied Digital Polaris Forge 1 Ready-for-Service milestone, MARA/HIF Matagorda powered-site acquisition, and Coravel/ACS/GIP 140 MW DFW hyperscaler agreement provide benchmarks for MW/GW-scale demand signals, while the BIS UAE action adds a policy channel to monitor for approved AI-chip/server recipients and follow-on U.S./UAE infrastructure orders. Site-control announcements without tenants remain lower-confidence demand evidence than signed leases or delivered-load milestones; high-quality secondary reports without primary economics should be verified against company filings/releases before promotion to the highest-confidence tier.
