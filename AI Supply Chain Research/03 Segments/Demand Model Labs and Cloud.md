# Demand, Model Labs, and Cloud

## Role in chain
Demand formation, training/inference workloads, cloud capex, GPU leasing, and custom silicon roadmaps.

## Key entities
Public: MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META, ORCL, NAVER, SK Telecom. Private/material: OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, CoreWeave, Lambda, Crusoe, Mistral, named/unnamed AI developers, unnamed hyperscaler/global-technology tenants when contract/lease/MW terms are primary or high-quality sourced, sovereign AI factory customers where MW/GW and funding terms are disclosed, and potential HPC tenants for power-rich campuses when filings or credible industry sources disclose GW-scale site/control rights. Public datacenter developers/former bitcoin miners with signed AI leases, AI cloud contracts, or SEC-filed project financing now include WULF, GLXY, IREN, APLD, CLSK, and HUT.

## Signals to monitor
- Capex revisions and datacenter lease commitments.
- GPU/accelerator cluster announcements with source-quality checks.
- Cloud instance availability and pricing.
- Model-training scale claims; inference cost/latency claims.
- Customer concentration and financing structures for neo-clouds.

## Open questions
- Does AI capex translate into revenue/margins or overbuild?
- Which custom silicon programs reduce merchant GPU share, and when?

## Current assessment

Frontier model-lab, GPU-cloud, hyperscaler, sovereign/Gulf, and Korea sovereign-AI demand is converting into policy-backed procurement pathways, long-duration datacenter leasing commitments, AI cloud services contracts, delivered capacity, project-finance pricing, hyperscaler balance-sheet funding, and competition for power-rich site pipelines. NVIDIA's Korea releases add primary evidence of large planned sovereign AI factory demand through NAVER's proposed 200 MW DSX expansion / 1 GW path and SK Telecom's up-to-2 GW Vera Rubin DSX plan under SK/NVIDIA LOIs. These sit alongside Alphabet's Q2 2026 hyperscaler capex/lease datapoint, the TeraWulf/Anthropic lease, Galaxy/CoreWeave Helios delivery and Phase II $3.507B / 9.875% financing pricing, Applied Digital/unnamed hyperscaler Delta Forge 1, Polaris Forge 3, and Delta Forge 2 leases totaling 810 MW critical IT load, CleanSpark/confidential global technology tenant Sandersville lease, Hut 8/confidential high-investment-grade tenant Beacon Point Phase 2 lease, IREN $2.8B AI cloud contracts, Applied Digital Polaris Forge 1 Ready-for-Service milestone, MARA/HIF Matagorda powered-site acquisition, and Coravel/ACS/GIP 140 MW DFW hyperscaler agreement. LOIs, nonbinding term sheets, and site-control announcements remain lower-certainty demand evidence than signed leases, contracted AI cloud services, direct hyperscaler capex/lease disclosures, closed financings, or delivered-load milestones.
