# Demand, Model Labs, and Cloud

## Role in chain
Demand formation, training/inference workloads, cloud capex, GPU leasing, and custom silicon roadmaps.

## Key entities

## Signals to monitor
- Capex revisions and datacenter lease commitments.
- GPU/accelerator cluster announcements with source-quality checks.
- Cloud instance availability and pricing.
- Model-training scale claims; inference cost/latency claims.
- Customer concentration and financing structures for neo-clouds.
