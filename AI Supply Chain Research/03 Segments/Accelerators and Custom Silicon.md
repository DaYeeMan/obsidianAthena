# Accelerators and Custom Silicon

## Role in chain
Core compute engines for training and inference. Demand is shaped by performance/watt, software ecosystem, memory bandwidth, networking, availability, and total cost of ownership.

## Key entities
NVDA, AMD, INTC, AVGO, MRVL, ARM, hyperscaler internal silicon teams, Cerebras, Groq, Tenstorrent and other private accelerators when material.

## Signals to monitor
- Product launches, shipment timing, allocation, and lead times.
- HBM attach rates and package complexity.
- Custom ASIC wins/losses and hyperscaler silicon roadmaps.
- Software ecosystem adoption and benchmark credibility.
- Export-control SKU changes.

## Current assessment
