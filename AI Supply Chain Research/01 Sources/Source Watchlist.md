# Source Watchlist

## Primary / high-quality sources

### Company IR, press releases, and blogs

- NVIDIA investor relations and newsroom
- AMD investor relations and newsroom
- Broadcom investor relations
- Marvell investor relations
- TSMC investor relations and monthly revenue releases
- Samsung Electronics IR
- SK Hynix IR
- Micron IR
- ASML IR
- Applied Materials IR
- Lam Research IR
- Tokyo Electron IR
- KLA IR
- Supermicro IR
- Dell IR
- HPE IR
- Arista Networks IR
- Vertiv IR
- Eaton IR
- Schneider Electric IR
- Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta, Oracle IR and cloud blogs

### Regulatory / policy

- US BIS export-control announcements and Federal Register notices
- SEC filings and 8-Ks/10-Qs/10-Ks where relevant
- Dutch/Japanese semiconductor equipment export-control announcements
- Taiwan, Korea, Japan industrial-policy and trade-ministry releases where relevant

### Industry / technical sources

- TrendForce public releases
- ServeTheHome
- DatacenterDynamics
- The Register datacenter/AI infrastructure coverage
- Cloud provider engineering blogs
- Semiconductor and datacenter conference materials when public

## Secondary sources to treat cautiously

- Unsourced social media claims
- Promotional vendor blogs without customer/source evidence
- News articles summarizing rumors without primary sourcing
- Benchmark claims without workload details, power envelope, or reproducibility

## Daily scan query themes

- AI accelerator shipments, lead times, allocation, export-control SKUs
- HBM qualification/capacity/pricing/yield
- CoWoS/advanced packaging capacity and ABF substrates
- Datacenter power, cooling, interconnection, permits, transformer/switchgear lead times
- AI server order/backlog/margin/customer concentration
- Ethernet/InfiniBand, 800G/1.6T optics, switch ASICs
- Hyperscaler capex revisions and cloud AI capacity availability
- China export controls, local substitution, inventory behavior
