# Event: NVIDIA announces Korea AI factory partnerships with NAVER/Brookfield and SK Group

## Facts

- NVIDIA published two primary newsroom releases on July 24, 2026 announcing proposed Korea AI infrastructure partnerships at the AI Summit.
- NAVER, NVIDIA, and Brookfield announced a proposed expansion of Korea sovereign AI factory infrastructure. The plan would expand NAVER's initial NVIDIA DSX AI factory deployment at the GAK Sejong hyperscale data center from 55 MW to 200 MW by 2028, with NAVER stating a path toward 1 GW of NVIDIA AI infrastructure.
- NVIDIA said it plans to invest $1 billion into NAVER Corp. Brookfield entered a nonbinding term sheet to fund up to $9 billion, with NAVER funding remaining amounts. NVIDIA's planned investment is subject to customary closing conditions and NAVER finalizing at least $9 billion of committed financing separate from NVIDIA's investment.
- The NAVER expansion is expected to use NVIDIA Vera Rubin and NVIDIA Blackwell platforms and to support Korea- and U.S.-based AI innovators with multi-tenant production-scale AI compute.
- SK Group and NVIDIA separately announced plans for a $500-billion-plus initiative spanning AI factories and next-generation memory. The parties signed letters of intent to formalize the agreement, so this should be treated as a planned/LOI-stage program rather than a final binding contract.
- SK Telecom plans to build a 2 GW NVIDIA Vera Rubin DSX AI Factory / AI Cloud in Korea, with the first AI factory planned to come online in 2027.
- NVIDIA and SK Hynix announced a long-term AI memory partnership intended to secure stable supply and codevelop/optimize next-generation AI memory, including HBM, for training, agentic AI, and physical AI workloads.

## Affected supply-chain nodes

- Sovereign AI / GPU cloud demand: Korea gains two large planned AI factory vectors, NAVER at 200 MW by 2028 with a stated 1 GW path and SK Telecom at up to 2 GW.
- Accelerators and systems: the programs explicitly reference NVIDIA DSX, Vera Rubin, Blackwell, and full-stack AI factory architecture.
- HBM and memory: SK Hynix is positioned as a long-term NVIDIA AI-memory/HBM partner for Vera Rubin-scale infrastructure.
- Datacenter power/construction/cooling: 200 MW and 2 GW planned AI factory loads would require substantial power, electrical, cooling, networking, and construction execution; NAVER's GAK Sejong site provides an identified initial location, while SK execution details need further validation.
- Infrastructure capital: Brookfield's nonbinding term sheet and NVIDIA's proposed $1B NAVER investment are capital-formation signals, but not yet closed financing.

## Interpretation

- This is a high-signal demand and capacity-intent event because it moves sovereign AI infrastructure from generic policy ambition toward named platform partners, stated MW/GW scale, financing parties, and platform/memory supply-chain dependencies.
- The announcement supports the current model that AI compute deployment bottlenecks are shifting toward powered datacenter capacity, financing, and HBM/platform supply, not only accelerator chip availability.
- Evidence quality is primary, but contractual certainty is below an executed lease/project-finance closing: SK's release references letters of intent, and NAVER/Brookfield financing is conditional/nonbinding until committed funding and closing conditions are satisfied.
