# Event: Riot signs 191 MW Rockdale lease with a leading frontier AI lab and delivers initial AMD capacity

## Facts

- Riot Platforms filed a Form 8-K on August 10, 2026 furnishing Q2 2026 results and an investor presentation.
- Riot said it executed a Data Center Lease and Services Agreement with one of the world's leading frontier AI labs for 191 MW of critical IT capacity at Riot's Rockdale, Texas campus.
- The lease has a 20-year initial term running through June 2048 and is expected to generate approximately $9.1 billion of total initial contract revenue.
- The tenant has two five-year extension options; Riot says total potential contract value would be approximately $16.1 billion if both extensions are fully exercised.
- Riot says phased delivery is expected to begin with 96 IT MW in December 2027 and reach the full 191 IT MW by June 2028, using Riot's existing fully approved Rockdale interconnection.
- Riot estimates cumulative NOI of $7.3-$8.2 billion over the base term and average annual NOI of $365-$411 million.
- Riot disclosed a $573 million interim financing facility from Morgan Stanley to fund initial development costs while the investment-grade credit backstop is finalized.
- The Q2 release says Riot completed delivery of the initial 25 critical IT MW to AMD on time and on budget, with the second 25 critical IT MW expansion under construction.
- Riot said the AMD lease plus the new frontier-AI-lab lease bring contracted Rockdale critical IT capacity to 241 MW and approximately $9.8 billion of long-term contracted revenue with two significant AI ecosystem companies.
- The investor presentation says the AMD 25 MW expansion is under construction, with initial 10 MW Ready for Service expected in November 2026 and remaining 15 MW expected in May 2027.
- Riot also disclosed that commercial and design discussions for the full Corsicana site are under a non-binding LOI with a single tenant; this is lower-certainty pipeline evidence because no signed lease/economics are disclosed.
- CNBC reportedly identified Anthropic as the tenant, but the durable evidence in this note is Riot's primary SEC-furnished disclosure; tenant identity remains officially undisclosed.

## Affected supply-chain nodes

- Demand / model labs: a frontier AI lab is committing to long-duration critical IT capacity at a power-rich former bitcoin-mining campus; the customer remains unnamed in primary filings.
- Datacenter power and construction: Riot converts approved/energized Rockdale power into a signed 191 MW critical-IT-load buildout with disclosed delivery timing, financing bridge, and NOI expectations.
- Accelerator / AI systems: the same Rockdale campus now has AMD contracted capacity and initial AMD MW delivery, making it another node where accelerator vendors and model labs are competing for deployable power and rack-scale infrastructure.
- Infrastructure suppliers: phased Tier 3 build-to-suit delivery, direct-to-chip / dense compute requirements, substation work, and long-lead equipment procurement create read-through for electrical, cooling, construction, and AI server/rack suppliers, though no supplier names beyond AMD and Morgan Stanley are disclosed.

## Interpretation

- This is high-signal because Riot disclosed a signed lease, 191 MW of critical IT load, 20-year term, $9.1 billion base-term revenue, phase delivery timing, financing support, and concurrent AMD delivery progress.
- The event adds RIOT to the direct public-market exposure set for AI datacenter lease monitoring alongside WULF, GLXY, APLD, CLSK, HUT, CORZ, BTDR, and IREN.
- Delivery and financing risk remain material: the first phase is not expected until December 2027, the credit backstop is still being finalized, and Riot does not disclose the tenant name, final project financing terms, equipment vendors, GPU/server mix, or rent commencement mechanics.
- The Corsicana LOI is useful pipeline context but should be weighted below signed leases because it is explicitly non-binding.
