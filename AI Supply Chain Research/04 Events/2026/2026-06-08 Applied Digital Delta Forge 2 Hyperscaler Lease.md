# Event: Applied Digital signs 210 MW Delta Forge 2 hyperscaler lease

## Facts

- Applied Digital filed an 8-K on June 9, 2026 with Exhibit 99.1 announcing that it had entered into a new long-term lease agreement at Delta Forge 2, a purpose-built AI Factory campus in a new southern U.S. state.
- The tenant is described as a U.S.-based high investment-grade hyperscaler; Applied Digital did not name the customer in the release.
- The agreement covers 210 MW of critical IT load under a 15-year take-or-pay structure with renewal options.
- Applied Digital says the lease represents approximately $5.2 billion of base-term contracted revenue, or approximately $12.7 billion if all renewal options are exercised over a 30-year total term.
- Applied Digital says this brings its total contracted portfolio to approximately $36 billion across five AI Factory campuses.
- Initial operations at Delta Forge 2 are anticipated to commence in Q1 2028.
- The release says the campus integrates Applied Digital's proprietary waterless cooling technology and high-power-density infrastructure for large-scale AI training and inference workloads.

## Interpretation

- This is another primary-sourced MW-scale AI datacenter procurement signal, similar in mechanism to TeraWulf/Anthropic and Galaxy/CoreWeave, but with an unnamed hyperscaler and a later Q1 2028 initial-operations target.

- Because the customer is unnamed and operations are not expected until Q1 2028, the event is less operationally de-risked than Galaxy's delivered/rent-commencement milestone.
