# Event: Coravel launches ACS-GIP datacenter platform with 140 MW hyperscaler customer at Dallas-Fort Worth campus

## Facts

- Data Center Dynamics reported on July 16, 2026 that Global Infrastructure Partners and ACS launched Coravel, a datacenter platform targeting hyperscalers.
- DCD says Coravel claims a 1.7 GW development portfolio, equal to about 1.2 GW of IT load, across the U.S., Europe, and Australia.
- DCD says about 150 MW is currently under construction.
- DCD says Coravel announced its first major hyperscaler customer agreement at its Dallas-Fort Worth campus, covering approximately 140 MW of critical IT load with approximately 100 MW of future expansion capacity.
- DCD says ACS subsidiary Turner Construction will lead delivery of the campus.
- Delivery timelines were not disclosed in the DCD article.
- Coravel's website is live and describes the company as a datacenter platform engineered for delivery; the site supports the platform identity but did not expose the detailed MW/customer fields in the fetched page text during this review.
- This event was discovered as a historical database gap on July 19, 2026; no Discord alert was sent solely because the cron discovered it after the publication date and the detailed MW/customer fields were validated from high-quality industry reporting rather than a directly parsed primary release.
