# Event: Galaxy launches $3.5B Helios Phase II financing for 260 MW CoreWeave AI datacenter build

## Facts

- Galaxy Digital filed a Form 8-K on July 22, 2026 announcing that Galaxy Helios Data Centers II LLC, an indirect wholly owned subsidiary, intends to offer $3.507 billion of senior secured notes due 2031 in a private Rule 144A / Regulation S offering.
- Galaxy says the net proceeds are intended to finance part of the development and construction of two buildings containing eight data halls with combined 400 MW utility capacity and 260 MW critical IT capacity on about 260 acres in Dickens County, Texas, and to fund debt-service reserves.
- Exhibit 99.1 identifies the project as Helios Data Center / Project Speed Phase II: 400 gross MW / 260 MW critical IT load leased to CoreWeave.
- The investor presentation says Helios has 1.63 GW of approved electrical capacity and 800 gross MW of leased capacity at Helios, including Phase II, all with CoreWeave as tenant.
- The presentation describes the Phase II lease as 260 MW critical IT load under contract, 15-year base term plus two five-year CoreWeave extension options, 13.7% starting MRC gross yield on cost, $10.4 billion of minimum contracted lease payments, Q2 2027 initial targeted rent commencement, 3.0%-5.0% annual rent escalator, and near-triple-net structure with about 90%+ NOI margin including operating expenses of $15/kW/month.
- Galaxy notes CoreWeave is focused on GPU-accelerated AI and HPC workloads, had $99.4 billion of contracted revenue backlog as of Q1 2026 per public filings, and is backed by NVIDIA, which owns about 11.5% equity and is obligated to purchase up to $6.3 billion of residual cloud capacity through April 2032.
- The financing is subject to market conditions and may not be completed on the described terms or at all.
- Galaxy filed a follow-up Form 8-K on July 24, 2026, reporting that Galaxy Helios Data Centers II priced the same $3.507 billion senior secured notes due 2031 at a 9.875% coupon; the offering is expected to close on July 28, 2026, subject to market and other conditions.
- The July 24 pricing release repeats that proceeds will finance part of the development and construction of the two-building / eight-data-hall Phase II project with 400 MW utility capacity and 260 MW critical IT capacity, and will fund debt-service reserves.

## Affected supply-chain nodes

- Datacenter power and construction: converts the already-leased Helios Phase II capacity toward a financed construction project with disclosed MW, rent timing, lease term, economics, and now priced debt terms.
- GPU cloud demand: CoreWeave remains the tenant for the 260 MW critical-IT-load Phase II project, reinforcing AI/HPC capacity absorption beyond the previously delivered Helios Phase I.
- Electrical/cooling/construction suppliers: Phase II financing can translate into orders for power distribution, cooling, construction, and commissioning equipment if the note offering closes and construction proceeds.

## Interpretation

- This is higher signal than a generic future campus announcement because it links tenant, critical IT load, lease economics, initial rent timing, and a dedicated financing package; the July 24 pricing moves the event from launch/market-test toward executable project financing, though closing remains pending.
- It reinforces the database model that power-ready campuses with credible tenants and financing are becoming a direct public-market route into AI infrastructure demand.
- The 9.875% coupon is a useful cost-of-capital datapoint for AI datacenter project finance: it supports construction conversion but also raises the bar for execution, lease economics, and tenant-credit underwriting.
- The main caveat is execution/financing risk: the follow-up 8-K says closing is expected July 28, 2026 subject to market and other conditions, and Exhibit 99.1 cautions that the offering may not be consummated.
