# Event: TeraWulf signs 401 MW Anthropic lease at Justified Data Campus

## Facts

- TeraWulf filed an 8-K on July 6, 2026 saying subsidiary Raylan Data LLC entered into a 20-year lease agreement with Anthropic PBC for approximately 401 MW of critical IT load for high-performance computing operations at TeraWulf's Justified Data Campus in Hawesville, Kentucky.
- The 8-K says capacity delivery is expected in phases beginning in late 2027 and concluding in early 2028; rent begins upon delivery of the applicable leased premises and continues for 20 years, with Anthropic holding options for up to two additional five-year renewals.
- TeraWulf's Exhibit 99.1 press release says the lease is expected to generate approximately $19 billion of contracted revenue over the initial term and that payment obligations are expected to be supported by an investment-grade credit.
- The same filing says TeraWulf agreed to sell its entire 50.1% ownership interest in the Abernathy Joint Venture to an investor group led by Fluidstack for approximately $530 million, monetizing capital for redeployment into wholly owned AI infrastructure.
- Data Center Dynamics independently covered the announcement as a $19 billion, 20-year Anthropic lease for a Kentucky data center.

## Interpretation

- The 401 MW load commitment and late-2027/early-2028 delivery window reinforce the view that usable AI compute is increasingly gated by power, site readiness, construction, and electrical/cooling supply chains, not just accelerator availability.
