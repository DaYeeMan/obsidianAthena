# Event: Applied Digital reports FY2026 AI Factory financing, portfolio, and power updates

## Facts

- Applied Digital filed a Form 8-K on July 27, 2026 with Exhibit 99.1 announcing fiscal Q4 / FY2026 results and operational updates.
- The company said recent highlights included a completed $2.15 billion private offering of 6.750% senior secured notes due 2031, issued at 98% of par through APLD ComputeCo 2 LLC, with proceeds to fund development of 200 MW of critical IT load at Polaris Forge 2 in Harwood, North Dakota.
- Applied Digital said it closed a revolving credit facility of up to $550 million, consisting of $350 million committed plus a $200 million accordion, arranged by Goldman Sachs and maturing in May 2029 to support pre- and post-lease development across campuses. Subsequent to quarter-end, the company said the revolver was upsized to $430 million committed with $120 million of accordion remaining.
- The company said it enhanced credit quality on existing CoreWeave leases through a restructured SPV subsidiary, unconditional springing guarantees from CoreWeave, Inc., and a $50 million letter of credit, following CoreWeave's investment-grade A3-rated refinancing.
- Applied Digital said it closed a $300 million Goldman Sachs-led bridge facility to support the fourth building at Polaris Forge 1. Subsequent to quarter-end, the company closed $1.59 billion of 7.000% senior secured notes due 2031 at par through APLD ComputeCo 3 LLC; proceeds were used to repay the $300 million bridge facility and will fund construction of the fourth building, representing 150 MW at Polaris Forge 1.
- Subsequent to quarter-end, Applied Digital entered a memorandum of understanding with CoreWeave to assign the Polaris Forge 1 Building 4 lease to a CoreWeave subsidiary upon achieving an investment-grade credit rating.
- Applied Digital said it had executed long-term leases representing approximately 1,410 MW of contracted critical IT load across five campuses: Polaris Forge 1, 2, and 3 in North Dakota, plus Delta Forge 1 and 2 in Louisiana and another southern state.
- The company said the portfolio represents approximately $36 billion of contracted revenue over initial 15-year base lease terms, or approximately $86 billion if all renewal options are exercised.
- Applied Digital said Polaris Forge 1's first 100 MW became operational in October 2025 and that Phase 1 of Building 2 delivered 75 MW on June 30, 2026, bringing total live capacity at the campus to 175 MW.
- The company said additional buildings at Polaris Forge 1, along with Polaris Forge 2, Polaris Forge 3, Delta Forge 1, and Delta Forge 2, are in various stages of construction.
- Applied Digital reported Q4 FY2026 HPC Hosting revenue of $203.0 million, including $44.1 million of base rent, $152.4 million of tenant fit-out services, and $6.5 million of tenant recoveries.
- Applied Digital said it is actively marketing another 1.7 GW of capacity across multiple states.
- The company said it is working with Base Electron Corp., an independent power producer that has engaged Babcock & Wilcox to develop approximately 1.2 GW of front-of-the-meter natural-gas-fired generation in the Dakotas in collaboration with regional utilities. Applied Digital said its shareholders own approximately 10% of Base Electron through Applied Digital's investment in Base Electron.
- Applied Digital also said it completed the separation of its cloud-services business by combining it with Ekso Bionics Holdings to form ChronoScale Holdings, an independent publicly traded accelerated-compute platform trading on Nasdaq under CHRN; Applied Digital owned approximately 96% of ChronoScale at fiscal year-end.

## Affected supply-chain nodes

- AI datacenter development and project finance: APLD's leases are moving from headline contracted capacity into funded construction packages with named note proceeds for specific MW blocks.
- GPU-cloud / model-lab infrastructure demand: CoreWeave remains tied to Polaris Forge 1 and now has additional credit-support language and a potential Building 4 assignment path.
- Datacenter power: APLD is pairing contracted campus capacity with a strategic generation initiative in the Dakotas, involving Base Electron and Babcock & Wilcox.
- Electrical, cooling, construction, and power-generation suppliers: funded and under-construction MW creates potential order pull-through for power train, cooling, construction, and generator/equipment vendors, but supplier names beyond Babcock & Wilcox were not disclosed.

## Interpretation

- This update is more than routine earnings: it adds project-finance execution, credit enhancement, and power-supply detail around a 1.41 GW contracted critical-IT-load portfolio that is already represented in the database through individual lease and delivery events.
- The $2.15 billion and $1.59 billion secured-note financings reduce uncertainty that signed leases convert into physical AI datacenter capacity, but they also expose APLD to cost-of-capital and construction-execution risk.
- The Base Electron / Babcock & Wilcox generation item is an early signal that AI datacenter developers may need more direct power-development relationships, not just utility interconnection rights.
