# Event: Applied Digital delivers 75 MW at Polaris Forge 1 Building 2

## Facts

- Applied Digital announced on July 1, 2026 that it achieved Ready for Service for Phase 1 of Building 2 at Polaris Forge 1 in Ellendale, North Dakota.
- The company says the milestone delivered 75 MW of operational AI capacity to its customer on schedule.
- Applied Digital says total live capacity at Polaris Forge 1 is now 175 MW.
- Applied Digital says Polaris Forge 1 is a fully leased AI Factory campus and is contracted to deliver 400 MW of critical IT load under long-term lease agreements at full buildout.
- Management framed the delivery as evidence of a repeatable model for converting power into operational AI capacity, not just securing sites or leases.
- This event was discovered as a historical database gap on July 15, 2026; no Discord alert was sent solely because it was not newly announced that day.

## Interpretation

- This is higher quality than a future lease announcement because it is an operational Ready-for-Service milestone: 75 MW moved from contracted/planned capacity into live AI capacity.
- The event reinforces the active thesis that power-ready campuses and execution capability are becoming a binding layer between accelerator availability and usable AI compute.
- It also reduces, but does not eliminate, execution uncertainty around Applied Digital's ability to deliver Polaris Forge capacity on schedule.
