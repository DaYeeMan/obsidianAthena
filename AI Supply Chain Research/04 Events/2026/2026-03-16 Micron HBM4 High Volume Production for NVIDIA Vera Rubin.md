# Event: Micron HBM4 in high-volume production for NVIDIA Vera Rubin

## Facts

- Micron's March 16, 2026 press release says it is in high-volume production of HBM4 36GB 12H designed for NVIDIA Vera Rubin.
- Micron says volume shipment of HBM4 36GB 12H began in calendar Q1 2026.
- Micron states the HBM4 product achieves over 11 Gb/s pin speeds, bandwidth greater than 2.8 TB/s, about 2.3x the bandwidth of its HBM3E counterpart, and greater than 20% power-efficiency improvement versus HBM3E.
- Micron also says it has shipped HBM4 48GB 16H samples to customers, which would increase capacity per HBM placement by 33% versus the 36GB 12H offering.
- Micron's HBM product page, last modified June 9, 2026, continues to highlight HBM4 36GB 12H as in high-volume production.

## Source links

- Primary: Micron press release, March 16, 2026: https://investors.micron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-high-volume-production-hbm4-designed-nvidia-vera-rubin
- Primary: Micron HBM product page: https://www.micron.com/products/memory/hbm

## Affected supply-chain nodes

- HBM4 supply and qualification for next-generation NVIDIA AI platforms.
- Advanced HBM stacking and packaging, including 12-high production and 16-high sampling.
- Accelerator platform transition from current-generation HBM3E-based systems toward Vera Rubin-era memory bandwidth and power envelopes.

## Interpretation

- This is primary evidence that Micron has moved beyond HBM4 sampling into production shipment for a named NVIDIA next-generation platform.
- The event supports treating Micron as a credible incremental HBM4 supplier rather than only an HBM3E-cycle participant, but it does not by itself quantify share, yield, pricing, or allocation versus SK Hynix and Samsung.
- If Vera Rubin platform ramps accelerate, HBM4 availability, advanced packaging throughput, and customer qualification cadence remain potential bottlenecks.
