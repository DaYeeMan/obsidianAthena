# Event: TSMC Q2 results confirm strong leading-edge/HPC demand and higher 2026 outlook

## Facts

- TSMC released Q2 2026 results on July 16, 2026.
- TSMC reported Q2 revenue of NT$1,270.38 billion / US$40.20 billion, up 36.0% year over year in NT dollars and 33.7% year over year in US dollars.
- TSMC said Q2 was supported by strong demand for leading-edge process technologies.
- Advanced technologies, defined as 7nm and below, accounted for 77% of wafer revenue in Q2 2026; 3nm accounted for 30%, 5nm for 33%, 7nm for 11%, and 2nm for 3%.
- TSMC's management report shows HPC represented 66% of Q2 net revenue and increased 20% sequentially.
- TSMC guided Q3 2026 revenue to US$44.6-45.8 billion and said the quarter should be supported by continued strong demand for leading-edge process technologies, including a steep ramp-up of 2nm.
- TSMC's presentation said management now expects 2026 revenue to increase by slightly above 40% in US-dollar terms.
- TSMC reported Q2 capital expenditures of NT$496.00 billion, up from NT$350.76 billion in Q1 2026 and NT$297.22 billion in Q2 2025.
- No Discord alert was sent: this is a primary-sourced material model update from a scheduled earnings release, but it is not a discrete new capacity contract, export-control shock, or operational bottleneck inflection beyond the existing foundry/AI-demand watchlist.

## Affected supply-chain nodes

- Leading-edge foundry capacity and allocation: TSMC 2nm/3nm/5nm/7nm.
- AI accelerator and custom silicon supply chain: merchant GPUs and hyperscaler ASICs that rely on leading-edge wafers and advanced packaging.
- Semiconductor equipment and materials demand: elevated capital expenditures are a follow-through signal for capacity additions, though the disclosure does not split wafer fabs versus advanced packaging.

## Interpretation

- The release reinforces the existing model that AI/HPC demand is still pulling hard on leading-edge foundry capacity rather than showing evidence of near-term digestion.
- The strongest supply-chain signal is the combination of 66% HPC revenue mix, 20% sequential HPC growth, 77% advanced-node wafer revenue, steep 2nm ramp language, and full-year revenue growth guidance slightly above 40%.
- The event does not by itself prove whether CoWoS/advanced packaging is easing or tightening; TSMC's public documents in this release do not quantify advanced-packaging capacity, utilization, or lead times.

## Investment / quant relevance

### Discretionary catalyst angle

- Positive read-through for TSMC and upstream equipment/material suppliers exposed to leading-edge/HPC capacity expansion; indirect confirmation for AI accelerator/custom-silicon demand at NVDA, AMD, AVGO, and MRVL.
