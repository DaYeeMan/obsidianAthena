# Event: Eaton reports accelerating electrical orders/backlog and raises guidance with data centers a key growth driver

## Facts

- Eaton filed a Form 8-K on July 31, 2026 furnishing Exhibit 99.1 with Q2 2026 results.
- Eaton reported record Q2 2026 sales of $8.5 billion, up 21% year over year, including 14% organic sales growth, above the high end of guidance.
- Segment margins were 23.1%, above the high end of guidance; Electrical Americas margins improved 190 bps sequentially.
- Eaton said 12-month rolling average orders were up 41% in Electrical Americas, 33% in Electrical Global, and 17% in Aerospace, driven by broad end-market strength.
- Eaton said total backlog grew 43% year over year in the Electrical sector and 28% in Aerospace.
- Electrical Americas sales were a record $4.0 billion, up 18% organically; operating profit was a record $1.1 billion; Electrical Americas total backlog at the end of June was up 33% year over year.
- Electrical Global sales were a record $2.5 billion, up 44% year over year including 18% organic growth and a 25% contribution from Boyd Thermal in its first full quarter post-acquisition; Electrical Global backlog was up 103% year over year.
- Eaton said the rolling 12-month book-to-bill ratio for the Electrical businesses remained strong at 1.2.
- CEO Paulo Ruiz said data centers remain a key growth driver and that strong performance and sustained demand led Eaton to raise full-year organic growth guidance.
- Eaton raised full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $13.40-$13.60, up 12% at the midpoint versus 2025, and guided full-year organic growth of 11%-13%.
- Eaton's Q2 2026 Form 10-Q disclosed total backlog of approximately $24.1 billion at June 30, 2026, with about 71% targeted for delivery in the next 12 months.

## Interpretation

- Eaton gives a second same-week primary supplier-side confirmation, after Vertiv, that datacenter/electrical demand is converting into orders, backlog, margins, and guidance.
- The strongest signal is not generic AI enthusiasm; it is the combination of accelerated electrical orders, 43% Electrical-sector backlog growth, 1.2x electrical book-to-bill, raised organic growth guidance, and explicit data-center growth-driver language.
- The disclosure is not AI/datacenter-only; Eaton cites broad end-market strength and does not quantify the data-center share of orders/backlog. This should be treated as high-signal supplier evidence but not as a pure-play AI datacenter order datapoint.
