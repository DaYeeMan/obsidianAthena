# Event: nVent reports record Q2 2026 growth and expands liquid cooling manufacturing for datacenter demand

## Facts

- nVent filed a Form 8-K on July 31, 2026 furnishing Exhibit 99.1 with Q2 2026 results.
- The release says reported sales were $1.5 billion, up 53% year over year, and organic sales grew 47%.
- Reported EPS was $1.32, up 103%, and adjusted EPS was $1.45, up 69%.
- Operating income was $301 million, up 92%; adjusted operating income was $323 million, up 61%; adjusted return on sales was 21.9%, up 110 bps.
- The Systems Protection segment reported $1.072 billion of Q2 sales, up 70%, with 62% organic growth and adjusted ROS of 23.2%, up 150 bps.
- CEO Beth Wozniak said nVent saw "significant data center growth," that new products contributed more than 30 points to sales growth, and that nVent announced another manufacturing expansion for liquid cooling to meet continued data center demand.
- nVent raised full-year 2026 guidance: reported sales growth to 37%-39% from prior 26%-28%, organic sales growth to 32%-34% from prior 21%-23%, GAAP EPS to $4.29-$4.39 from prior $3.68-$3.78, and adjusted EPS to $5.00-$5.10 from prior $4.45-$4.55.
- The Q2 Form 10-Q says the megatrends of electrification, sustainability, and digitalization, including increased use of artificial intelligence, have led to sales growth, particularly in the infrastructure vertical, which includes nVent's data centers business primarily in Systems Protection.
- This event was discovered as a historical database gap on August 8, 2026; no Discord alert was sent solely for the delayed discovery.
