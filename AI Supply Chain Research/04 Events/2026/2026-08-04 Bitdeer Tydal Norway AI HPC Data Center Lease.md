# Event: Bitdeer announces $4.7B 16-year AI/HPC data center lease for Tydal, Norway campus

## Facts

- Bitdeer AI, part of Bitdeer Technologies Group, announced on August 4, 2026 that subsidiary Tydal Data Center AS executed a 16-year colocation lease and services agreement with Volta Tydal AS, a subsidiary of Volta.
- The Tydal, Norway campus commitment covers 121 MW of IT load, supported by an estimated 133 MW of gross power.
- The release says the entire 121 IT MW will be configured to run NVIDIA GPUs for the end customer, described as a leading AI lab; Dell Technologies is named as the technology provider.
- The agreement represents approximately $4.7 billion of contracted revenue over the initial 16-year base term. A one-time 8-year renewal option would increase potential total contract value to approximately $8.0 billion over 24 years.
- Lease economics are described as a modified-gross arrangement averaging approximately $202/kW/month over the first 16 years, with electricity costs reimbursed by the tenant on a pass-through basis.
- Volta's obligations are anticipated to be supported by letters of credit arranged by affiliates of J.P. Morgan and another top-tier global financial institution, totaling approximately $1.3 billion and subject to customary conditions.
- Bitdeer says the Tydal AI data center is expected to be among Norway's largest AI data centers upon completion, with an estimated PUE of approximately 1.1 and 100% renewable energy.
- The StockTitan/GlobeNewswire release summary says Phase 1 targets December 31, 2026 and about $500 million of capex remains. Bitdeer affiliates retain 100% ownership of the Tydal campus and no Bitdeer equity or warrants were issued in connection with the transaction.
- Bitdeer filed a Form 6-K on August 3, 2026 for an auditor-related disclosure; this monitor did not find a same-day SEC-filed lease exhibit at the time of review. Treat the company press release as the evidence base until a 6-K/exhibit, investor deck, or contract summary is filed.
