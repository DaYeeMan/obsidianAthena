# Event: Bitdeer announces $4.7B 16-year AI/HPC data center lease for Tydal, Norway campus

## Facts

- Bitdeer AI, part of Bitdeer Technologies Group, announced on August 4, 2026 that subsidiary Tydal Data Center AS executed a 16-year colocation lease and services agreement with Volta Tydal AS, a subsidiary of Volta.
- The Tydal, Norway campus commitment covers 121 MW of IT load, supported by an estimated 133 MW of gross power.
- The release says the entire 121 IT MW will be configured to run NVIDIA GPUs for the end customer, described as a leading AI lab; Dell Technologies is named as the technology provider.
- The agreement represents approximately $4.7 billion of contracted revenue over the initial 16-year base term. A one-time 8-year renewal option would increase potential total contract value to approximately $8.0 billion over 24 years.
- Lease economics are described as a modified-gross arrangement averaging approximately $202/kW/month over the first 16 years, with electricity costs reimbursed by the tenant on a pass-through basis.
- Volta's obligations are anticipated to be supported by letters of credit arranged by affiliates of J.P. Morgan and another top-tier global financial institution, totaling approximately $1.3 billion and subject to customary conditions.
- Bitdeer says the Tydal AI data center is expected to be among Norway's largest AI data centers upon completion, with an estimated PUE of approximately 1.1 and 100% renewable energy.
- The StockTitan/GlobeNewswire release summary says Phase 1 targets December 31, 2026 and about $500 million of capex remains. Bitdeer affiliates retain 100% ownership of the Tydal campus and no Bitdeer equity or warrants were issued in connection with the transaction.
- Bitdeer filed a Form 6-K on August 5, 2026 furnishing Exhibit 99.1 with the company press release for the Tydal lease. This upgrades the evidence base from wire-only company release to SEC-furnished primary company evidence; a full lease agreement or contract summary has still not been filed.
- Bitdeer filed two additional Form 6-Ks and a prospectus supplement on August 10, 2026. The incorporated 6-K summarizes amended/key Tydal terms: the tenant has a no-fee termination right at 10 years, Phase 1 targets December 31, 2026, Phase 2 targets March 31, 2027, both lease and services fees have 3% annual escalators, the company has the right to terminate if Volta misses milestones related to the credit backstop, and remaining capex is still approximately $500 million.
- Bitdeer's August 10 Q2 2026 Form 6-K / Exhibit 99.1 frames Tydal as the first large-scale proof point for its colocation strategy and discloses a broader power-infrastructure table: Tydal Phase 3 adds 47 gross MW targeted for H2 2027 for colocation / AI Cloud, Rockdale TX 563 MW is in active evaluation for crypto-to-colocation / AI Cloud transition, Knoxville TN 86 MW has been redesigned for larger-scale customer demand with Q3 2027 targeted completion, and Wenatchee WA 13 MW has AI data-center design documents / building permit application submitted while Bitdeer plans to accommodate the latest NVIDIA GPUs. These are pipeline signals, not signed AI leases.
- Bitdeer also filed a $1.0 billion at-the-market ordinary-share prospectus supplement on August 10, 2026. That improves available funding flexibility for Tydal and other AI infrastructure capex but introduces potential dilution and does not by itself prove project-finance closure or LC finalization.

## Affected supply-chain nodes

- AI datacenter capacity: 121 MW IT / 133 MW gross power in Norway committed to an AI/HPC tenant stack.
- Accelerator deployment: the full IT load is described as configured for NVIDIA GPUs, with a leading AI lab as end customer.
- AI server / systems integration: Dell Technologies is named as technology provider.
- Datacenter power: the campus uses renewable local hydropower, dual grid connectivity, and an estimated PUE of approximately 1.1; Norway becomes a more visible European AI factory location.
- Financing / credit support: the lease includes expected $1.3B LC support arranged by J.P. Morgan affiliates and another global financial institution, subject to conditions.
- Funding / dilution: Bitdeer has a $1.0B ATM program available after the August 10 prospectus supplement; this is useful capex funding optionality but not the same as non-dilutive project finance.

## Interpretation

- This is a high-signal same-day AI datacenter lease because it combines named public exposure, MW scale, long-term contracted revenue, GPU configuration, AI-lab end-customer language, credit support, expected capex, and delivery timing.
- The event expands the active AI campus dataset beyond U.S. power-rich former crypto miners and hyperscalers into European renewable-power AI factories. It also adds BTDR as a direct public exposure to the AI datacenter lease theme.
- The disclosure is less granular than SEC-filed lease exhibits: Volta's end customer is unnamed, GPU generation/count, firm procurement status, detailed construction schedule, conditions precedent, and LC finalization are not disclosed.
