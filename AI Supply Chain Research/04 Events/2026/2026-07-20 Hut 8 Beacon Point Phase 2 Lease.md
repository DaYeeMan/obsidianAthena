# Event: Hut 8 signs second 352 MW Beacon Point AI data center lease

## Facts

- Hut 8 filed a Form 8-K on July 20, 2026 announcing commercialization of the second phase of its Beacon Point data center campus in Nueces County, Texas.
- Exhibit 99.1 says the second phase is a 15-year, triple-net lease for 352 MW of IT capacity with the same high-investment-grade company that executed the Phase 1 lease.
- The second lease doubles the tenant's contracted Beacon Point capacity to 704 MW and brings the campus-level base-term contract value to $19.6 billion.
- Hut 8 says the new lease has $9.8 billion of base-term contract value over the 15-year base lease term, inclusive of a 3.0% annual base-rent escalator, and expected cumulative NOI contribution of $9.8 billion / average annual NOI of $655 million upon stabilization.
- The company says Beacon Point is fully commercialized against 1,000 MW of utility capacity secured under an interconnection agreement with AEP Texas for electric delivery service.
- Hut 8 says it will deliver a second 352 MW AI factory designed to NVIDIA's DSX reference architecture for gigawatt-scale AI infrastructure, supported by 500 MW of utility capacity.
- Initial Phase 2 data hall delivery is expected in Q2 2028.
- Across Hut 8's AI data center portfolio, contracted IT capacity rises to 949 MW, supported by 1,330 MW of utility capacity, with aggregate base-term contract value of $26.6 billion and average annual NOI of more than $1.75 billion.
- Hut 8 says 100% of contracted AI data center capacity is leased to or backstopped by investment-grade counterparties; the tenant remains unnamed.
- Site preparation is underway, long-lead critical equipment has been procured, and initial energization remains on schedule for Q1 2027.

## Affected supply-chain nodes

- AI datacenter demand and leasing: another public, primary-sourced MW-scale lease by an unnamed high-investment-grade tenant.
- Datacenter power and interconnection: 1,000 MW Beacon Point utility capacity tied to an AEP Texas interconnection agreement; no incremental utility capacity required for Phase 2 per Hut 8.
- Electrical, cooling, and construction supply chain: 352 MW additional AI factory phase with long-lead critical equipment already procured and initial delivery targeted for Q2 2028.
- AI systems architecture: Hut 8 references NVIDIA DSX reference architecture, but the filing does not quantify GPU/server procurement or named equipment suppliers.
