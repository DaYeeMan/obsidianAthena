# Event: Applied Digital signs 300 MW Delta Forge 1 hyperscaler lease

## Facts

- Applied Digital filed a Form 8-K on April 23, 2026 with Exhibit 99.1 announcing a lease agreement with a new U.S.-based high-investment-grade hyperscaler at Delta Forge 1, a 430 MW AI Factory campus.
- The lease covers 300 MW of critical IT load purpose-built for the hyperscaler's AI and HPC infrastructure.
- Applied Digital says the lease represents approximately $7.5 billion of total contracted value over an estimated 15-year lease term.
- The customer is Applied Digital's second U.S.-based investment-grade hyperscaler across three AI Factory campuses and its third hyperscale tenant overall.
- Applied Digital says total contracted lease revenue increased to more than $23 billion, with more than 50% backed by investment-grade customers.
- Initial operations at Delta Forge 1 are anticipated to commence in mid-2027.
- The company also said it expected to enter up to $300 million senior secured bridge financing for Polaris Forge 1 Building 3 and up to $300 million senior secured revolving credit facility for platform development.
- This event was discovered as a historical database gap on July 26, 2026; no Discord alert was sent solely because the event is not a new same-day development.
