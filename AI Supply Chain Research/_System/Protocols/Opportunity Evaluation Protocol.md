# Opportunity Evaluation Protocol

## Minimum bar

An opportunity must have a plausible connection to public-market fundamentals, expectations, revisions, sentiment, positioning, or a testable quant research hypothesis.

Reject generic AI news, unsupported rumors, and items without supply-chain mechanism.

## Required fields

- What changed?
- Who is affected?
- Supply-chain mechanism.
- Public-market exposure.
- Evidence and source quality.
- Confidence.
- What would verify or falsify the thesis?
- Discretionary catalyst angle, quant-testable angle, or both.

## Candidate types

- Bottleneck forming or easing.
- Capacity expansion or delay.
- Supplier/customer relationship change.
- Capex revision or procurement signal.
- Regulatory/export-control shift.
- Pricing power or margin inflection.
- Logistics, power, cooling, construction, or materials constraint.

## Quant-test framing

For quant ideas, define:

- universe;
- signal definition;
- timestamp/source availability;
- expected lead/lag;
- target variable;
- backtest design;
- transaction-cost/liquidity concerns;
- failure modes.
